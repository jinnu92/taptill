What TapTill is, in one minute

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant
  • Rider
  • TapTill runs on the café’s main computer (the counter computer). Bills, menu and reports are stored there.
  • It works without internet. Phones and tablets connect over the café Wi-Fi.
  • Everyone has their own name and PIN. What you can see depends on your role.
  • The menu on the left (or the bar on top) shows only the screens your role can use.

Tip: Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) to search for any screen or setting.

What the plan tags mean

Some features depend on the licence. A tag shows the lowest plan that has it: Advanced Pro Pro Plus. No tag means every plan has it. The 14-day trial has everything.

With a Custom licence, features are picked one by one. To see yours, the Admin opens Settings → Licence → What your plan includes.

If something is missing: a locked screen says Available in your plan upgrade — contact your seller. Nothing is broken; ask the owner.

How your café serves

Pick how guests order once. After that every table and order shows one button for the next step, and the Alerts bell tells the right person when it is their turn.

  • Cashier
  • Waiter

Counter, takeaway, delivery and advance orders are not part of any area. They always work as pay at the counter and collect on Pickup.

Areas, pay timing and extras (Admin)

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → How you serve. A café starts with one area, Dining. Add, rename or hide areas (for example Indoor and Terrace) and move each table into one area.
  2. Pick one of the four setups for each area. The same page has Pay first or Pay at the end for Cashier does everything and Guests order by QR. The other two have a fixed pay time.
  3. Pay first or at the end: in those two areas the cashier can change a single order in POS with the Pay now / Pay later button beside the order buttons. On a new order in a pay-first area, Pay later saves it as pay-at-the-end and sends it to the kitchen.
  4. QR as an extra: any area can also let guests order by QR on top of its own setup. It switches on by itself when you make QR codes for the tables, and you can turn it off here.
  5. Print the bill as soon as a bill is asked for: tick it to print at once on the counter printer. The cashier’s alert then says Take payment.
  6. A change applies to new orders. Orders already open finish the way they started.

Waiters and “Cashier does everything”: waiters do not see tables in those areas, even after you hire waiters. Switch the area to Waiters at tables once you have waiters.

Plans: the QR setup and QR extra need Table QR self-ordering Pro. On a plan without it, a QR area works as Cashier does everything. Waiters taking orders on phones needs phones and tablets Advanced; waiters can always use the counter computer.

The Alerts bell

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  1. The bell with a red count is on POS, Tables and waiter phones. It chimes once when something new arrives.
  2. Each alert has one button: Served (food ready), Print bill or Take payment (bill asked), Accept or Take payment (QR order), Done (waiter called), Handed over (collect order ready), Send out (your own delivery is ready: opens Pickup to pick the rider) or Picked up by rider (a Swiggy or Zomato order ready). It disappears when done.
  3. Waiters see alerts for their areas. Cashiers see everything. In a café with no waiters, cashiers get the Served alerts too.
  4. An alert that waits too long chimes again and turns amber Pro.
  5. Serve, Bill asked and the Alerts bell are in every plan.

How the team works together

Most jobs pass from one person to the next. Each flow below shows who does what, what the next person sees, and how they know it is their turn.

Dine-in: from table order to free table

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen

This is the “Waiters at tables” setup. Other setups change who takes the order or when it is paid; see How your café serves. The button on each table card always shows the next step.

  1. WaiterOpens the table, adds items, Send to kitchen
  2. KitchenGets a KOT or a ticket, taps Start, then Ready
  3. WaiterAlerts bell chimes: Served
  4. WaiterBill asked, the cashier is alerted
  5. CashierPrint bill, then Take payment (split or UPI)
  6. TapTillThe table shows Free again
  1. Waiter: on the phone or at the counter, open Tables and tap a free table, then Take order (or press F3 in POS). Add the items and press Send to kitchen (Ctrl+S).
  2. Kitchen, how they know: it depends on Settings → Printing & kitchen → Orders go to the kitchen by:
    • Printed ticket (KOT): a KOT prints on the counter printer. A KOT from a phone is printed by the counter computer, so TapTill must be open and logged in there.
    • Kitchen screen: a new ticket appears on the kitchen screen within a few seconds (with a chime when Sound on).
    • Screen and printed ticket: both.
  3. Kitchen: tap Start when cooking begins and Ready when the food is done. The ticket turns amber, then red, if it waits too long.
  4. Waiter, how they know: the Alerts bell chimes once and shows Served with the table name. The table card says Cooking while you wait and Ready with a Serve button when the food is done. Tap Serve after the food reaches the guests. Items added after serving, even after the bill was asked for, bring the Serve step back.
  5. More items: use ⋯ → Add items on the table card, add the items and press Send to kitchen. Only the new items go on the next KOT or ticket.
  6. Bill: the guest asks for the bill (or taps Ask for bill on the table QR page). The waiter taps Bill asked. The cashier is alerted with Print bill (F7). A guest’s QR request alerts the cashier the same way.
  7. Cashier: tap Print bill on the alert, then Take payment (F9): pick Cash, UPI or Card, or Split. The bill prints and the table becomes Free. If the Admin turned on Print the bill as soon as a bill is asked for, the bill is already printed and the alert says Take payment.

Remember: order takers cannot take payment. The bill always goes through a Cashier or the Admin.

Counter or takeaway: pay first, collect when ready

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Kitchen
  1. CashierBills on Counter and takes payment
  2. KitchenKOT or ticket with the order number, Start, Ready
  3. DisplayThe number shows under READY
  4. CashierHands it over, taps Handed over on Pickup
  1. Cashier: in POS keep Counter (F2), add items, press Pay. The bill prints. With KOT printing on, the KOT prints too.
  2. Kitchen, how they know: a KOT, or a ticket on the kitchen screen labelled with the last 3 digits of the bill number (for example 001). Tap Start, then Ready.
  3. Customer, how they know: when the kitchen taps Ready, the number appears on the customer display Advanced. Without a kitchen screen, the cashier can press Ready (F6) in POS right after the payment, or Mark ready on Orders.
  4. Cashier: when the customer picks it up, open Pickup and tap Handed over on the order. The number leaves the display and the kitchen ticket is cleared.

Order at the counter, we serve: if the area uses this setup, the cashier picks the guest’s table before Pay (or Collect instead for a normal pickup). After payment the order goes to the kitchen; when it is Ready, the Alerts bell shows Served to the area’s waiters, and serving frees the table. These orders never appear on Pickup. See How your café serves.

Good to know: ready numbers drop off the display by themselves after 20 minutes.

Table QR self-order: guest orders from their phone Pro

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  1. GuestScans the table QR, taps Place order
  2. Cashier / WaiterChime, Alerts, Accept
  3. KitchenKOT or ticket, Start, Ready
  4. GuestCall waiter or Ask for bill
  5. StaffServed when ready; Done on calls; Print bill, Take payment
  1. Guest: scans the QR on the table, adds items and taps Place order. Their phone shows Waiting for the staff to confirm.
  2. Staff, how they know: on POS and Tables (and on waiter phones), the Alerts bell shows a red count, a chime plays and a message pops up (“New order from T6”). Anyone who takes orders (Admin, Cashier, Order taker) can open it.
  3. Staff: check the items and press Accept. The items join that table’s order and go to the kitchen. Or press Reject and pick a reason (for example Item sold out); the guest sees it.
  4. Auto-accept: if the Admin ticked Send table orders to the kitchen without a staff check (auto-accept), orders skip this step.
  5. Serve: when the kitchen taps Ready, Alerts shows Served. Take the food to the table and tap it.
  6. Calls: Call waiter rings the chime and shows in Alerts and on the table card (“Calling waiter”). Go to the table, then tap Done. Ask for bill alerts the cashier with Print bill, then Take payment.
  7. Pay first areas: if the area is set to pay first, the guest’s phone says Pay at the counter to start your order. The cashier’s alert shows Take payment ₹N; paying accepts the order and sends it to the kitchen. An order not paid within 30 minutes expires and the guest sees Expired — please order again.
  8. Paying: guests never pay on the phone. Pay at the counter as usual.

Swiggy or Zomato order Pro

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Kitchen
  1. PlatformNew order arrives, chime
  2. CashierOnline orders, Accept
  3. KitchenKOT or ticket, Start, Ready
  4. CashierPacks it, Picked up by rider on Pickup
  1. Cashier, how they know: the Online orders button on POS shows a red count and a chime plays. The Admin’s Today screen also lists waiting orders.
  2. Cashier: open Online orders, check the items and press Accept. TapTill makes the bill and sends it to the kitchen. Or press Reject with a reason.
  3. If an item says Not linked to your menu, Accept is greyed out. The Admin links it in Settings → Online orders → Menu mapping.
  4. Kitchen: cooks it like any order. Cashier: hands it to the rider and taps Picked up by rider on Pickup.

Status today: Swiggy and Zomato connect through UrbanPiper, which is marked “coming soon” in TapTill 2.2. Until then the Admin can press Send test order in Settings → Online orders to practise this flow. Orders you type in yourself with Delivery (F4) and a Swiggy or Zomato platform work in every plan.

Change or cancel after the KOT

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  1. WaiterRemoves an item from an open table, tells the kitchen
  2. Cashier / AdminCancels a whole bill with a reason
  3. KitchenThe ticket disappears from the screen
  4. AdminChecks Discounts & cancellations
  1. One item less on an open table: anyone who takes orders can open the table, press − or × on the line and save. TapTill does not print a cancel slip, so tell the kitchen yourself.
  2. Cancel a whole order or a paid bill: only a Cashier or the Admin. Open Orders, tap the bill, press Cancel order, type the reason (it is required) and press Cancel order again. Order takers do not see this button.
  3. Kitchen, what they see: a cancelled order’s tickets leave the kitchen screen. A printed KOT is not taken back, so say it out loud.
  4. Money: a bill cancelled on the same day simply drops out of the day’s sales. A bill paid on an earlier day shows as a refund in the cash drawer on the day you cancel it.
  5. Admin: every cancellation, with who did it and why, is in Reports → Discounts & cancellations Advanced and the Audit log.

A shift: clock in, open the drawer, close the day

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  1. All staffClock in / out on the login screen
  2. First cashierCounts the opening cash
  3. CashierPayouts during the day
  4. CashierClose day, Z-report prints
  5. AdminChecks the Z-report and shifts
  1. Everyone Advanced: on the login screen tap Clock in / out, tap your name and type your PIN. This does not log you in. Do it again when you leave.
  2. First Cashier or Admin of the day, how they know: right after logging in, TapTill asks Start the day: count the cash in the drawer. Count the notes and press Save opening cash. A cashier cannot skip this; an Admin can.

  5. Admin, how they know: Today shows “Day closed. Z-report is ready”. Check over or short cash on Reports → Day-end and the hours on Service → Shifts. Only the Admin can Reopen day.

Getting started (Admin)

Do these once, in this order, before the first customer. Most of it is in Settings, which has its own list of sections on the left.

Install TapTill and open it

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Download the file for your computer from the Download page.
  2. Windows: open TapTill-…-Setup.exe. If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Follow the installer and open TapTill from the Start menu.
  3. Mac: open the .dmg and drag TapTill into Applications. If the Mac says it cannot be opened, click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Android tablet: open the Download page on the tablet, tap Download for Android and open the .apk. Allow Install unknown apps if asked, then tap Install. See Run the café on an Android tablet.
  5. Install Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge too, so bills print in one tap without a print window.

Good to know: your data stays in the TapTill folder on this computer and is kept when you update, reinstall or uninstall.

Run the café on an Android tablet

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  1. One Android tablet (Android 8 or newer) can be the whole till: billing, menu, reports and backups all run on it. No laptop is needed.
  2. The first time you open TapTill, tap Allow for notifications and Allow when asked to let TapTill always run in the background. This keeps the till, waiter phones and the kitchen screen working when the screen turns off.
  3. Leave the tablet on its charger during service. A small TapTill is running notification stays at the top: that is normal.
  4. Printer is optional. Go to Settings → Printer & KOT → This tablet's printer and choose Off (no printer), Bluetooth receipt printer (pair it first in the tablet's Bluetooth settings) or Android print dialog. Press Save printer, then Print test receipt.
  5. Waiter phones and a kitchen screen connect to the tablet like to a laptop: turn on Settings → Phones & tablets and pair them on the same Wi-Fi.
  6. The tablet plays a different sound for a new online or QR order, a table calling a waiter, food ready, and an approval or accountant question. Change or mute each sound in the tablet's Settings → Apps → TapTill → Notifications.
  7. Exports (CA export, Excel, backups) are saved in the tablet's Downloads → TapTill folder.

Licence: the tablet has its own Installation ID in Settings → Licence. If the tablet is reset to factory settings, the ID changes: ask your seller to move the licence (it is free).

Create the Admin name and PIN

  • Owner / Admin
  1. The first time TapTill opens it shows Welcome to TapTill.
  2. Type Your name, choose a PIN of 4 to 6 digits and type it again.
  3. Press Create Admin and start.

Tip: make a second Admin for a trusted partner later (on Users). If the only Admin forgets the PIN, your seller has to reset it.

Trial and the licence key

  • Owner / Admin
  1. The first 14 days are a free trial with every feature. The banner on top shows the days left.
  2. To buy, open Settings → Licence, press Copy next to the Installation ID and send it to your seller on WhatsApp.
  3. Paste the key you get back (it starts with CPOS) in Licence key, or use or open a key file. Press Install key.
  4. What your plan includes lists your features. Limits shows how many users, phones and outlets you can have.

Renewing: do the same with the new key before the end date. Bills and menu are never touched.

Café name, address, GSTIN and bill numbers

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Cafe & invoice.
  2. Fill Cafe name, Phone, GSTIN, FSSAI, Address and the Receipt footer (for example “Thank you! Visit again.”). Add a logo with Upload logo. Press Save cafe details.
  3. Under GST invoice, add the Legal name, State, State code and Default HSN/SAC. With a GSTIN, paid bills print as a Tax Invoice. Press Save GST invoice.
  4. Under Bill numbering, set the Bill number prefix. Tick Restart numbers every financial year for numbers like CAFE/25-26/00001.

GST extra or included in the price

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Tax & GST.
  2. Pick No GST, GST extra (exclusive) or GST included (inclusive).
  3. Set the GST rate (%), for example 5. Tick Round off bill total to nearest rupee if you want whole rupees.
  4. Press Save tax. The bill shows CGST and SGST as two halves.

One item at another rate? See Different GST rate or HSN for one item.

Payment methods and your UPI QR

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Payments & UPI. Cash, UPI and Card are there already. Add others (for example “Paytm card”) and press Save payments.
  2. Type your UPI ID (for example mycafe@okaxis) and Payee name, then press Save UPI.
  3. Now the pay screen, the unpaid bill and the customer display show a QR with the exact amount. Tick Also print the QR on paid receipts if you want it there too.

Important: TapTill does not check the bank. The cashier confirms only after the money arrives on the phone.

Discounts, delivery platforms and advance orders

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Billing.
  2. Discounts: tick Allow discounts on bills (on by default) or untick it to stop all discounts.
  3. Delivery platforms: the names shown for delivery orders (Own, Swiggy, Zomato). Aggregators are platforms that collect the money themselves, so their orders stay out of the cash drawer.
  4. Advance orders Advanced: how many minutes before pickup the kitchen gets the order, and whether the advance is refunded when an order is cancelled.

Add categories and items

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Menu. Under Categories, type a name (for example Coffee) in New category and press Add. The number next to each category sets its order on the POS.
  2. Tap a category. At the bottom of the items, type the New item name and price, tick or untick Veg, and press Add item.
  3. To change a name or price, tap it and type. It saves when you leave the box.
  4. Sold out today? Untick Available. Not selling it any more? Press Archive (Show archived brings it back with Restore).
  5. Hide on a category takes the whole category off the POS.

Sizes, add-ons and choices

  • Owner / Admin
  1. On Menu, under Options & variants, press New option group.
  2. Give it a Name (Size, Add-ons, Milk). For a size, tick Required (a variant such as Size) and keep Max picks at 1. For add-ons, set Max picks to how many a guest may choose.
  3. Type each option and its price change in ₹ (0 = same price). + Add option adds a row. Press Save group.
  4. Attach the group: press Options on the item, tick the group, press Save.

Kitchen notes like “less sugar” need no setup: the cashier types them when adding the item.

Different GST rate or HSN for one item

  • Owner / Admin
  1. On Menu, press Options on the item.
  2. Under GST, type the HSN/SAC and pick the GST rate (Default uses the café rate).
  3. Press Save.

Good to know: combos are taxed at your default GST rate under HSN/SAC 996331. A ! badge on Options means the item’s rate is no longer a GST slab: pick a new one.

Upload the whole menu from Excel Advanced

  • Owner / Admin
  1. On Menu, press Download template (or Export menu to start from your current menu).
  2. Fill it in Excel and save it as .xlsx.
  3. Press Import from Excel and choose the file. TapTill shows a preview: new, changed, unchanged and rows with errors (skipped).
  4. Optionally tick Archive items not in this file. Press Confirm import.

Add, rename or hide tables

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Tables & QR ordering.
  2. Under Tables, type a New table name (T1, Garden 2) and press Add table.
  3. Use Rename or Hide on a table; hidden tables get a Restore button.

Printer, receipts and KOT

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Install your 80 mm or 58 mm thermal printer with its driver and make it the default printer on the computer.
  2. Open Settings → Printing & kitchen. Pick the Paper width and number of Copies.
  3. Tick Print bill automatically after payment and, if the kitchen uses paper tickets, Enable kitchen order tickets (KOT). Press Save printer.
  4. Under Kitchen, choose Orders go to the kitchen by: Printed ticket (KOT), Kitchen screen or Screen and printed ticket. Press Save kitchen.
  5. After your first bill, press Print test receipt to check.

Good to know: the kitchen screen choices need the kitchen display in your licence Advanced. Orders sent by an order taker always print a KOT for the new items.

Add staff, roles and PINs

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Users. Under Add a person, type the Name, pick the Role, type a PIN (4 to 6 digits) twice and press Add.
  2. Give each person their own PIN. Never share one login: reports and the audit log show who did what.
  3. In the people list: Edit changes the name or role, Reset PIN sets a new PIN, Turn off stops a person who left (their bills stay).

Limits: the badge shows Users: 3 of 6. A role your plan lacks shows (Not in your plan). Store manager and Accountant need Pro, Kitchen needs the kitchen display Advanced.

Auto-lock when nobody is using it

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Staff & lock.
  2. Set Lock the screen after this many idle minutes (0 = never on this laptop). Press Save.

Good to know: phones and tablets always lock, after 10 minutes when this is 0. A locked screen asks for the same person’s PIN, or tap Switch user.

Pair a phone or tablet Advanced

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Phones & tablets. Tick Allow phones & tablets on the Wi-Fi, then close TapTill and open it again.
  2. Press Pair new device. A 6-digit code and an address appear.
  3. On the phone, join the café Wi-Fi and open that address (or scan the QR). If the browser warns about the certificate, see Phone shows a “not secure” warning.
  4. Type the code, give the device a name (for example “Waiter phone 1”), choose Staff phone or tablet, Customer display screen or Kitchen screen, and press Pair this device.

Good to know: the code works for 5 minutes, once. Put customers on your router’s guest Wi-Fi. Remove takes a lost phone off at once.

Set up the kitchen screen Advanced

  • Owner / Admin
  • Kitchen
  1. In Settings → Printing & kitchen, set Orders go to the kitchen by to Kitchen screen or Screen and printed ticket.
  2. Set Turn amber after and Turn red after (minutes). Press Save kitchen.
  3. On the kitchen tablet, pair it as a Kitchen screen (no login needed). Or add a Kitchen user on Users; they log in and see only the kitchen screen.
  4. Tap Sound off once on the kitchen screen to turn the chime on.

Customer display and menu board Advanced

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Customer display. Set Seconds per slide, Menu highlights and tick Show “order ready” numbers.
  2. Menu board Pro: choose When there are no offers or Always (between offers), and tick the categories to show.
  3. Press Save display.
  4. Second screen on this computer: press Open display, drag the window to the second screen and press F11. TV or tablet on the Wi-Fi: pair it as a Customer display screen.

What it shows: live offers as slides, ready order numbers, and the UPI QR while the cashier is taking a UPI payment.

Set up table QR ordering Pro

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Turn on phones first (Pair a phone or tablet, step 1). Guests reach the menu over the café Wi-Fi.
  2. Open Settings → Tables & QR ordering. Press Print table QR codes and print the sheet (one card per table).
  3. Decide on Send table orders to the kitchen without a staff check (auto-accept) and press Save.
  4. A QR card lost or copied? Press New code next to that table: the old code stops working at once. New codes for all tables replaces every card.

Turn on loyalty points or stamps Pro

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Loyalty and tick Turn on loyalty (points or stamps).
  2. Pick Points (set Points earned per ₹100, 1 point is worth (₹), Minimum points to redeem) or Stamps (every Nth item free).
  3. Optional Birthday offer: percent off or one free item, once a year within 3 days of the birthday.
  4. Press Save loyalty settings.

Change the colours

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Appearance.
  2. Pick a theme (Espresso, Matcha, Berry, Ocean, Charcoal, Saffron) or your own Main colour, and a Mode: Light, Dark or Auto.
  3. Press Save. Reset to default goes back to Espresso.

Every day at the counter and tables

Log in, switch user and lock

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant
  • Rider
  1. Tap your name, then type your PIN on the pad (or on the keyboard) and press OK.
  2. TapTill opens your screen: Admin → Today, Cashier and Order taker → POS, Kitchen → Kitchen, Store manager → Stock, Accountant → Accounts, Delivery → My deliveries.
  3. Leaving the counter? Press Switch user so the next person logs in as themselves.

  • Owner / Admin
  1. TapTill backs up every day by itself. Set the time in Settings → Backups & data → Backup time and how many to Keep last.
  2. Add an Extra backup folder (a USB drive or a Google Drive / OneDrive folder) and press Test folder, then Save backup settings.
  3. Backup now makes one at once. Download backup saves a copy anywhere you like.

If something goes wrong: Today shows “The last backup failed”. Check the extra folder (is the USB plugged in?).

Restore a backup

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Open Settings → Backups & data → Restore from backup. Choose the file (and type its password for a .ttbak file).
  2. Press Check backup file. TapTill shows its orders, last bill number, menu size and users.
  3. Type RESTORE and press Restore. A safety copy of today’s data is saved first, then you log in again.

Careful: this replaces all current data. Open tables at that moment are lost.

Encrypted cloud backup Pro

  • Owner / Admin
  1. Install the Google Drive or OneDrive app on the computer.
  2. In Settings → Outlet & cloud backup → Cloud backup, press Find Google Drive folder or type the folder.
  3. Choose a password of at least 10 characters, type it twice and press Save cloud backup. Back up to cloud now tests it.

Keep the password safe. Without it the cloud copy cannot be opened, and nobody can recover it.

Install an update

  • Owner / Admin
  1. When the internet is on, TapTill looks for a new version by itself. The Admin sees a bar: TapTill 2.x.x is available. Your installed version is always shown at the bottom of the menu.
  2. Open Settings → Updates. Read What's new, then press Update now and confirm.
  3. TapTill downloads the update, checks it is genuine, backs up your data and installs it over the current version. It opens again by itself in a minute or two. Windows may ask “Allow this app to make changes?”: press Yes.
  4. On an Android tablet, Android asks Do you want to update this app?: press Update. The first time, allow Install unknown apps for TapTill when asked.
  5. No internet? Press Check now later, or download the new version from the TapTill website and install it over the old one: your data stays.

Tip: update after closing the day, not during the rush.

Jump anywhere: the search box

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Store manager
  • Accountant
  1. Press Ctrl K (Cmd K on a Mac) or tap Search in the menu.
  2. Type a few letters (“cash”, “gst”, “user”) and press Enter. It shows only what your role may open.

Troubleshooting and questions

Forgot PIN, or “Too many tries”

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant
  1. Staff: ask the Admin. They open Users, press Reset PIN next to your name and type a new one twice.
  2. Too many tries: after 5 wrong PINs you wait 60 seconds. After 15 wrong PINs in 30 minutes the wait is 15 minutes. The message says how long.
  3. The only Admin forgot the PIN: contact your seller. They can reset it on a copy of your data file.

“Your role can’t do this” or a missing button

  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant

Each role sees only its own screens (see the roles table). Ask the Admin, or ask a Cashier for payments and discounts. Order takers see Your role can’t do this. Ask a cashier. if they try to pay.

“Available in your plan upgrade — contact your seller”

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant

That feature is not in your licence. Nothing is lost: data you entered during the trial stays. The Admin can check Settings → Licence → What your plan includes and ask the seller for a higher plan or an add-on. A role your plan lacks shows (Not in your plan) on Users.

The printer is not printing

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  1. Check the printer is on, has paper and the lid is closed.
  2. Make sure it is the default printer on the computer, and that Chrome or Edge is installed.
  3. In Settings → Printing & kitchen, check the Paper width and press Print test receipt.
  4. Reprint the bill from Orders → Reprint once it works.

KOTs from phones: they print on the counter computer. If the counter shows … slips not printed — check printer, fix the printer; TapTill must be open and someone logged in there.

An item says “Unavailable”

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter

Someone unticked Available on the Menu (for example it ran out). The Admin ticks it again to sell it.

The internet is down

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  • Store manager
  • Accountant

Keep working. Billing, KOT, printing, tables, the kitchen screen, reports and backups all run on the counter computer. Phones and tablets keep working over the café Wi-Fi router even without internet. Only Swiggy/Zomato orders and the cloud backup upload need internet; they catch up later.

A phone or tablet can’t connect

  • Owner / Admin
  • Waiter
  • Kitchen
  1. The phone must be on the café staff Wi-Fi, not the guest Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  2. On the counter computer, TapTill must be open, and Allow phones & tablets on the Wi-Fi must be ticked (after ticking it, restart TapTill).
  3. Open the exact address shown in Settings → Phones & tablets. If the computer’s Wi-Fi address changed, the address changes too.
  4. A pairing code works 5 minutes, once. Press Pair new device for a fresh one. A removed device needs a new code.
  5. At the limit of your plan, a new pairing is refused: Remove an old device first.

Phone shows a “not secure” or certificate warning

  • Owner / Admin
  • Waiter

TapTill makes its own secure certificate for the café. Either accept the browser warning once (Advanced → Proceed), or install the certificate: in Settings → Phones & tablets → Phone certificate use iPhone / iPad profile or Certificate file (Android, others) and follow How to install it. The fingerprint must match on the phone.

Licence ending, grace days and read-only

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier
  • 15 days before the end date a banner says Licence ends on … (Hide for today hides it).
  • After the end date there are 7 grace days: Licence expired — … days left before read-only.
  • Read-only: no new bills, payments or menu changes; POS shows the message instead of Pay. Reports, reprints, exports and backups still work. Nothing is deleted.
  • Fix: the Admin sends the Installation ID to the seller and installs the new key in Settings → Licence. Billing works again at once.

“Your computer’s date is wrong — fix the date and time”

  • Owner / Admin
  • Cashier

TapTill stops billing when the computer’s clock goes back in time. Set the correct date, time and time zone (India) in the computer’s settings and reload TapTill.

Moving to a new computer

  • Owner / Admin
  1. On the old computer, close the day and press Download backup in Settings → Backups & data. Copy the file to a USB drive.
  2. Install TapTill on the new computer and create a temporary Admin.
  3. Restore the backup (Restore a backup). Your menu, bills and staff come back.
  4. Send the new computer’s Installation ID to your seller. They issue a key with the same end date.

Sending a problem to your seller

  • Owner / Admin

Open Settings → Help and press Create support file. It has logs, version and technical settings, but no orders, customers, menu or prices. Send it with a short note of what happened.

Training checklists

Sit with the new person and tick each line once they can do it alone. Ticks are saved in this browser only. Printing shows the selected role’s checklist and steps, without pictures.
