Billing at the counter

Counter, dine-in and delivery from one screen, at rush-hour speed.

  • Tap or type. Start typing an item name and press Enter, or tap a tile. Veg and non-veg marks on every tile.
  • Modifiers and variants. Sizes like S, M and L, add-ons like extra cheese, and a short note for the kitchen. They print on the KOT and the bill.
  • Split payment. Part cash, part UPI, part card on one bill, and the cash drawer adds it up correctly.
  • UPI QR on the bill. Add your UPI ID once; the bill and the customer display show a QR with the exact amount.
  • Advance orders Advanced with a pickup time and an advance payment. The kitchen ticket comes up before pickup, with a reminder at the counter.
  • Print the bill before payment for a table with F7, then settle it when the guest pays.
  • Discounts by amount or percent, each one recorded in the audit log.

GST bills, tax invoice and GSTR-1

Bills your accountant will accept, without retyping anything.

  • GST extra or included in your menu prices, with CGST and SGST shown on every bill and round-off to the rupee.
  • Tax invoice format with your legal name, GSTIN, state, HSN/SAC, taxable value per GST rate and the amount in words. Not registered? Print a “Bill of supply” instead.
  • Bill numbers by financial year, such as CAFE/25-26/00001, restarting on 1 April.
  • Per-item GST rate (0, 5, 12 or 18%) where an item needs a different rate.
  • GSTR-1 export: a monthly B2C summary by rate and an HSN summary, as CSV files.

Tables, kitchen and self-ordering

From the table to the kitchen to the pickup counter, without anyone shouting.

  • Tables at a glance: who is seated, the running bill, and whether the food is in the kitchen or being prepared.
  • Table service status Pro: seated, ordered, served and bill requested, with timers, so no table is forgotten.
  • Table QR self-ordering Pro: print a QR for each table. Guests order from their phone on the café Wi-Fi, call a waiter or ask for the bill. Staff accept each order before it goes to the kitchen, and a guest only ever sees their own table’s order.
  • KOT to the kitchen as a printed ticket, on a kitchen screen, or both. Only new items go on the next KOT.
  • Kitchen screen Advanced: cooks tap Start and Ready; tickets turn amber, then red, if they wait too long.
  • Customer display Advanced on any TV: your offers as a slideshow and ready order numbers along the bottom. Menu board Pro mode shows categories and prices in large type and hides sold-out items.

Reports, cash drawer and profit

The answers owners want at closing time, worked out on your own computer.

How licensing works

No online activation and no account. Your licence key works offline, is tied to your counter laptop, and carries your plan and any extra features you activate.

  1. InstallOn your counter laptop, Windows or Mac.
  2. TryEvery feature, free for 14 days.
  3. ChooseA plan. Send us the Installation ID from Settings → Licence.
  4. ActivatePaste the key we send back. It works offline on that laptop.
  5. RenewA new key before the end date. Bills and menu stay as they are.

You get reminders from 15 days before the end date, then 7 days of grace. After that TapTill becomes read-only: you can’t make new bills, but you can still open reports, reprint, export and back up. Nothing is ever deleted.

Changing laptops? Send us the new Installation ID and we issue a key for it with the same end date.

Questions owners ask

Does TapTill need internet?

No. Billing, KOT, printing, tables, the kitchen screen, reports and backups all run on your computer and keep running when the internet is down. You don’t need internet to install a licence key either.

Phones and tablets connect over your café Wi-Fi router, which does not need internet. We recommend a separate guest Wi-Fi for customers, so your staff devices and the counter computer sit on their own network. If you use table QR self-ordering, guests’ phones need to reach the counter computer over Wi-Fi, and we help you set that up.

Only Swiggy/Zomato orders (with your integration partner account) and cloud backup uploads need internet.

Where is my data stored?

On the computer where TapTill is installed, in a TapTill folder:

  • Mac: ~/Library/Application Support/TapTill
  • Windows: %APPDATA%\TapTill

This folder is kept when you uninstall or update TapTill. Backups are taken every day at a time you choose. TapTill doesn’t send your sales data anywhere; nothing leaves the computer unless you turn on cloud backup or online orders.

Do you take a commission on my sales?

No. TapTill charges no per-order or transaction fees. You pay for your plan and any extra features you switch on. If you take Swiggy or Zomato orders, those platforms and your integration partner have their own terms.

Is it GST ready?

Yes. Choose GST extra or included in price and your rate. Every bill shows CGST and SGST, your GSTIN and FSSAI, with round-off. The tax invoice format adds HSN/SAC, taxable value per rate and the amount in words, with bill numbers by financial year. Reports → GST exports the GSTR-1 B2C and HSN summaries as CSV for your accountant.

Which printers work?

80 mm or 58 mm USB thermal receipt printers that install as a normal printer on Windows or Mac (with the maker’s driver). Pick 80 or 58 mm in Settings → Printer & KOT. With Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge installed, TapTill prints bills and KOTs straight away without a print dialog. Phones and tablets send their prints to the counter laptop’s printer.

Can I get Swiggy and Zomato orders?

Yes, on Pro and above, or as an add-on on Advanced. Online orders arrive in TapTill to accept or reject and then go to the kitchen like any other order. This works through an integration partner, so you need your own partner account, and internet for those orders.

Is my data safe? What about backups?

Your data stays on your laptop. TapTill takes a backup every day at the time you choose and keeps the last 30, and can also copy it to a USB drive or a cloud-synced folder. Restoring takes a few clicks. On Pro and above, the encrypted cloud backup locks each copy with your password before your Google Drive or OneDrive app uploads it. Keep that password safe: without it the cloud copy cannot be opened. Uninstalling TapTill does not delete your data.

Can waiters use their phones?

Yes, on Advanced and above. Turn on Phones & tablets in Settings and pair each phone or tablet once with a code from the Admin. Waiters take table orders on the phone, and a tablet can be the kitchen screen or the customer display. Guests who scan a table QR only reach the ordering pages for their own table.

What do I need to run it?

Windows: a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 laptop or PC. Mac: a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) or an Intel processor; download the matching file. Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge is recommended for silent printing. A touchscreen is optional: everything also works with a keyboard and mouse.

Windows or Mac shows a security warning on first launch. Is that normal?

Yes. TapTill is new and not yet signed with a paid publisher certificate, so the system asks you to confirm once.

Windows (“Windows protected your PC”): click More info, then Run anyway.

Mac (“TapTill can’t be opened” or “Apple could not verify”): click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to TapTill, then confirm with your Mac password.

You only do this the first time.

What happens when the trial ends?

TapTill becomes read-only until you install a key: no new bills, but your sales, reports and backups are all still there. Reinstalling does not restart the trial. Send us your Installation ID and we send your key.
