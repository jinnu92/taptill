TapTill tour

    Billing

    Tap or type an item

    A counter order in a few seconds, by touch or keyboard.

    1. Start typing: “cap” and Enter adds Cappuccino. Arrow keys move the highlight.
    2. Or tap a tile. The badge shows how many are on the bill.
    3. Counter, Dine-in or Delivery, also with F2, F3 and F4.
    4. Change the quantity with + and −, or remove a line.
    5. Subtotal, CGST, SGST and round-off update as you add items.
    6. Pay with F9.

    Billing

    Pick a size and add-ons

    Items with options open a quick picker with big buttons.

    1. Required choices come first, such as Small, Medium or Large.
    2. Add-ons like extra cheese add their price to the item.
    3. Number keys 1–9 pick options; Enter adds the item.
    4. Add a short note for the kitchen, like “less sugar”. It prints on the KOT and the bill.

    Billing

    Pay, even split across methods

    Part cash, part UPI on one bill.

    1. Pick Cash, UPI or Card, or add more than one.
    2. Enter each amount; TapTill shows what is left to pay.
    3. The cash drawer and reports count each part under its own method.

    Billing

    The printed bill

    Straight to your 80 mm or 58 mm thermal printer, no print dialog.

    1. Your café name, address, phone, GSTIN and FSSAI on top.
    2. Items with quantity, rate and amount.
    3. CGST and SGST at your GST rate.
    4. The total, rounded to the rupee, and your thank-you line.

    Billing

    UPI QR with the amount

    Guests scan and pay the exact bill amount.

    1. Add your UPI ID once in Settings.
    2. The bill shows a QR with the exact amount and the bill number.
    3. The customer display can show it too. The cashier confirms the payment.

    Billing

    GST tax invoice and GSTR-1

    A tax invoice your accountant can use as it is.

    1. “Tax Invoice” with your legal name, GSTIN and state (or “Bill of supply” if you are not registered).
    2. HSN/SAC, taxable value per GST rate and the amount in words.
    3. Bill numbers by financial year, like CAFE/25-26/00001.
    4. Reports → GST: monthly B2C and HSN summaries as CSV files.

    Tables

    Tables at a glance

    See every table, its bill and its food, from the counter.

    1. How many tables are occupied and free.
    2. A busy table shows its running bill, item count and time seated.
    3. Kitchen status: in kitchen, preparing or ready.
    4. Tap a free table to start a dine-in order.

    Tables

    Table QR self-ordering

    Guests order from their own phone on the café Wi-Fi.

    1. Print a QR sheet with one code per table.
    2. Guests scan, pick items and place the order. They only see their own table’s order.
    3. Orders wait in the Self-orders inbox until staff accept them.
    4. “Call waiter” and “Ask for bill” chime at the counter.

    Kitchen

    Kitchen screen

    Orders appear the moment they are saved.

    1. Filter new, preparing and ready tickets.
    2. Each ticket shows the table or order number and how long it has waited.
    3. Tap Start when cooking begins…
    4. …and Ready when it is done.
    5. Ready tickets wait for pickup, and the number goes to the TV.

    Kitchen

    Customer display

    Any TV near the counter becomes your offer board and pickup screen.

    1. Live offers play as a slideshow.
    2. Combo price next to the regular price.
    3. Ready order numbers along the bottom, so nobody has to shout names.

    Kitchen

    Menu board

    The same TV can show your menu with prices. Change a price once and the board follows.

    1. Your menu in big, readable columns, straight from the Menu page.
    2. Veg and non-veg marks next to every item.
    3. Long menus turn pages by themselves, between your offers.

    Reports

    Dashboard

    How the café is doing, compared with the period before.

    1. Today, yesterday, 7, 30 or 90 days, or your own dates.
    2. Revenue, orders, average bill and items sold, with the change from before.
    3. Sales by hour: when the money comes in.

    Reports

    What sells when

    Plan your menu, offers and staff around real sales.

    1. Revenue and orders for each part of the day.
    2. The top five items in each time slot.
    3. Set your own time slots in Settings.

    Cash

    Cash drawer

    Know what should be in the drawer before you count it.

    1. Cash, UPI and card totals for the day.
    2. Expected cash: opening cash plus cash sales, minus payouts.
    3. Count notes by denomination and see any difference.
    4. Tick each UPI and card payment as you match your statement.

    Cash

    Z-report: close the day

    One printout with the whole day.

    1. Total sales with discounts, CGST, SGST and round-off.
    2. By payment method, order type and delivery platform.
    3. Counted cash against expected cash.

    Offers

    Offers and combos

    Combos, happy hours and buy X get Y, applied by the till.

    1. Create a combo, percent off, buy X get Y or a display-only promo.
    2. Each offer shows what it gives and when it runs.
    3. Live now or scheduled, at a glance.
    4. Edit, duplicate or archive an offer.

    Offers

    The till suggests the free item

    Cashiers never miss an offer, even on a phone.

    1. Two French Fries on the bill qualify for “Buy 2, get 1 free”.
    2. TapTill suggests the free item; one tap adds it.
    3. The total updates with the offer applied.

    Stock & profit

    Stock and recipes

    Stock comes off by itself as you sell.

    1. Add ingredients in g, ml or pieces, with a reorder level.
    2. Give each item a recipe; stock comes off when a bill is paid.
    3. Record purchases and wastage, and count stock to match the shelf.
    4. See the food cost % of each item.

    Stock & profit

    Expenses and profit

    What you actually made today and this month.

    1. Record expenses by category, or straight from a cash payout.
    2. Profit per day and month: sales minus food cost and expenses.
    3. A chart of profit over time.

    Loyalty

    A regular at the till

    Points, favourites and birthdays, from a phone number.

    1. Type the phone number and press Find: name, visits and favourites appear.
    2. A reminder when their birthday is near.
    3. Redeem points on this bill, or apply the birthday offer.

    Loyalty

    Customer list

    Everyone who came back, in one list.

    1. Each customer with visits, spend and last visit.
    2. Points or stamps balance.
    3. Export the list as CSV for your own messages.

    Settings

    Appearance

    Match the till to your café’s colours.

    1. Six colour presets, or your own brand colour.
    2. Light, dark or automatic.
    3. Preview before you save. Text stays readable whatever colour you pick.
